How I can help

Here for life's important paperwork

Hospitals & care facilities

Gentle bedside visits for patients and residents in hospitals, rehab centers, nursing homes, and assisted living.

At your home

Powers of attorney & healthcare papers

Vehicle titles

No surprises

Simple, fair fees

North Carolina law sets the most a notary may charge (N.C. Gen. Stat. § 10B-31). I never charge more, and you'll approve your total, including any mileage, in writing before I head your way.

Notary fees
Acknowledgment per signature $10
Jurat, verification or proof per signature $10
Oath or affirmation without a signature, per person $10
Travel actual mileage at the federal business rate, only if you agree in writing before I travel 72.5¢/mile

A friendly note

I am not an attorney licensed to practice law in North Carolina, and I may not give legal advice or accept fees for legal advice. I can't help you choose, prepare, or explain a document, so please check with an attorney for that.
