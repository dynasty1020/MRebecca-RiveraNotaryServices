North Carolina Fee Schedule

North Carolina law (N.C. Gen. Stat. § 10B-31) sets the most a notary may charge. I never charge more, and you'll approve the total, including any mileage, in writing before I travel.

Notarial Act Fee
Acknowledgment (per principal signature) $10.00
Jurat, verification or proof (per principal signature) $10.00
Oath or affirmation without a signature (per person) $10.00
Travel: actual mileage at the federal business rate, only if agreed in writing before I travel 72.5¢ / mile

About

Careful, Patient, and On Time

A notarization protects you against fraud and forgery. That's why I check every signer's identification, make sure they're signing willingly, and record each notarization in my journal.

Whether it's paperwork at a kitchen table or an urgent healthcare document at a hospital bedside, I take the time to get it right and treat every family with respect.

Careful ID Checks

A current government-issued photo ID for every signer, such as a driver's license, passport, or military ID.

Notary Journal
